INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

WHEREAS the Company wishes to engage Contractor; NOW THEREFORE the parties agree:

1. SERVICES AND REVISIONS. Contractor shall perform the services described in Exhibit A and shall make revisions to the Work Product until Company is satisfied, in Company’s sole discretion.
2. COMPENSATION. Company shall pay Contractor $75 per hour. Contractor shall invoice monthly and Company shall pay each invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. 
3. OWNERSHIP. All Work Product shall be “work made for hire” and is the sole property of Company. Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns all rights therein.
4. RESTRICTIVE COVENANT. For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in any business that competes with Company.
5. INDEMNIFICATION. Contractor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Company from all claims arising out of the Services. 
6. Contractor will be available during Company’s normal business hours and may not subcontract any portion of the Services.
7. If Contractor fails to meet a deadline, Contractor shall pay liquidated damages of $500 per day.
