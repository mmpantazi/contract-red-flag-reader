SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Services and Deliverables. Contractor will deliver the logo set and brand guide described in Schedule A. Additional work requires a written change order with a price.

2. Revisions. The fee includes two (2) rounds of revisions per deliverable. Further rounds are billed at $60/hour. Client feedback is due within 5 business days; silence is deemed acceptance.

3. Fees. The fee is $3,000: 40% deposit upfront and 60% on delivery. Invoices are payable within 14 days. Overdue amounts accrue interest of 1.5% per month.

4. Intellectual Property. Ownership of the final deliverables transfers to Client upon receipt of full payment. Contractor retains all rights in pre-existing materials, tools and general know-how, and grants Client a non-exclusive licence to use any such materials embedded in the deliverables.

5. Limitation of Liability. Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid under this Agreement, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

6. Termination. Either party may terminate on 14 days' notice. Client pays for all work performed up to the termination date.

7. Confidentiality. Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret for three (3) years after the end of this Agreement.
